1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences over time, which helps enhance your browsing experience.

Cookies are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide useful information to website owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several purposes to enhance your experience on 2048 Cupcakes:

  • Essential Functionality: To provide basic website functionality
  • Performance & Analytics: To understand how users interact with our site
  • Personalization: To remember your preferences and game progress
  • Advertising: To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Security: To protect against fraud and ensure website security

3. Types of Cookies We Use

🔒 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems.

  • Maintain user sessions
  • Remember game progress
  • Enable basic website functionality

📊 Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

  • Google Analytics for traffic analysis
  • Page load time monitoring
  • User behavior tracking

🎯 Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are relevant to you and your interests.

  • Google AdSense cookie management
  • Personalized ad delivery
  • Ad performance tracking

🔧 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as videos and live chats.

  • Remember user preferences
  • Customize website appearance
  • Language preferences

8. GDPR and Your Rights

If you are located in the European Union, GDPR gives you the following rights regarding cookies:

  • Right to Information: Clear information about cookies we use
  • Right to Consent: Consent before non-essential cookies are placed
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Change your cookie preferences anytime
  • Right to Access: Information about cookies stored on your device
  • Right to Object: Object to certain types of cookies

9. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner that allows you to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Customize your cookie preferences
  • Learn more about our cookie use

Your consent is stored for 12 months, after which you'll be asked to review your preferences.

10. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies, some features of our website may not function properly:

  • Game progress may not be saved
  • Personalized settings may be lost
  • Advertisements may be less relevant
  • Website performance analytics may be affected
