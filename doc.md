Indulge your sweet tooth and challenge your mind with **2048 Cupcakes**, the delightful twist on the classic 2048 puzzle game! In this charming version, instead of dull numbers, you’ll be merging colorful and delectable cupcakes on a vibrant 4x4 grid. Your mission? - **Cupcake Combination**: Slide cupcakes in any direction to merge those with the same design. Each successful combination creates a new cupcake with double the value, progressing you toward that delicious 2048 Cupcake.

- **Tile Generation**: After every move, new cupcakes appear on the grid, adding an exciting layer of strategy as you plan your next steps. Keep an eye out for new opportunities to merge!

- **Visual Appeal**: Enjoy an array of charming cupcake designs that make every move a visual treat. The colorful graphics enhance your gaming experience, making it suitable for players of all ages.
