Frequently Asked Questions

🎮 How do I play 2048 Cupcakes?
Use arrow keys to move cupcakes. When two identical cupcakes touch, they merge into one with double the value. The goal is to create the 2048 cupcake!
📱 Is the game available on mobile devices?
Yes! 2048 Cupcakes is optimized for both desktop and mobile devices. You can play on any modern web browser.
💾 Can I save my game progress?
Your game progress is automatically saved in your browser's local storage. However, clearing your browser data will reset your progress.
🐛 I found a bug! How do I report it?
Please email us at support@2048cupcakes.top with details about the bug, including your browser and device information.
🔒 Is my data safe?
Absolutely! We only collect minimal data needed to improve your gaming experience. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.
